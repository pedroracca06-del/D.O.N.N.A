# Strict OTE

Status: CURRENT
Authority: owner-approved

Strict OTE is one of the three current PRIME execution-model names.
It is a tightened model and must not be reduced to a generic Fibonacci retracement or a simple fib touch. A fib touch alone is not OTE.

Historical PROS or generic OTE material does not define the current model.

## Required sequence

1. **Liquidity event.** A meaningful liquidity event/sweep at or near the origin the leg draws from. Origin selection without a real sweep does not qualify.
2. **Displacement.** A displacement/impulse leg that breaks structure and proves intent/control. Drift or a slow grind is not displacement.
3. **Objective dealing range.** The range is drawn from the objectively identifiable sweep origin to the displacement extreme, not from a discretionary or convenient swing.
4. **Wick-to-wick fib.** The retracement fib is measured wick-to-wick: low-to-high for a long, high-to-low for a short. Body-to-body or mixed measurement does not qualify.
5. **OTE zone.** The optimal trade entry zone is the 0.618-0.786 retracement of that wick-to-wick range. 0.5 is the equilibrium reference only, not itself an entry zone.
6. **Confluence.** HTF/context and any relevant PD-array line up with the OTE zone; confluence sharpens the zone but does not replace it.
7. **Confirmation.** A PRIME reclaim/acceptance confirmation inside the OTE zone is required before entry; presence in the zone alone is not confirmation.
8. **Risk.** Risk is placed by structure — the invalidation point implied by the sweep/displacement/dealing-range read — not by a fixed tick or dollar offset.

## Boundaries

FVG is context/confluence only inside this model and is not an entry model on its own.
STDV extension/location is a companion read for target and context only; it is not a fourth execution model and does not replace the sequence above.

Detailed execution mechanics beyond this sequence remain subject to the separately approved canonical trading specification and owner review.
